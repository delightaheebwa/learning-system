# Newton's law of cooling

Newton's law of cooling says that the rate of temperature change is proportional to the current temperature difference from the surroundings:

- \(\frac{d\Delta T}{dt} = -k\Delta T\)

Its solution is exponential decay:

- \(\Delta T(t) = e^{-kt}\)

## Why it connects here

This is the same structural pattern as exponential growth: the derivative is proportional to the current value.

That is why the special role of \(e\) is useful in differential equations. Writing the solution with base \(e\) makes the proportionality constant appear directly in the exponent.
